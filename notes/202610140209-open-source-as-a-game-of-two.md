# Open source as a game of two

Two players. The author chooses once: to open the work or to close it. The reader chooses each
day: to check or to trust. That gives four cells.

| | the reader checks | the reader trusts |
|---|---|---|
| **the author opens** | the claim is tested | the claim could be tested |
| **the author closes** | there is nothing to check | trust is all there is |

Only the top row lets the reader change column. That is the whole case for it.

**Open question.** It is a picture, not a result. No payoff was measured, and a real game
needs them: what the author loses by opening, what the reader pays to check. The owner's
argument with its sources is the book *Closed Source: The God that Failed*, doi
[10.5281/zenodo.23333656](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23333656).
