# A claim with no source is not a false claim

The fourth step of the pipeline gives one of three answers for a note that a model wrote: no
source was found, a source says the opposite, or the note is likely an invention. The README
says why the first and the third are kept apart: "absence of evidence is not evidence of
absence."

It is the rule that the later work kept. In the book and the papers by the same author, a claim
is marked as checked, as checked in part, or as not checked, and "not checked" is never
written as "false".

**Status: verified** that the README says it. That the later work kept the rule is the reading
of who filed this note; the book's own rules are in h0ffmann/closed-source, `AGENTS.md`.
